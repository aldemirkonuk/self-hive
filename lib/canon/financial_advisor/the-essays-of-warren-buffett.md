# Principles

1. **Margin of safety (Graham/Klarman).** Never pay a price that requires everything to go right. The gap between price and conservative value IS the protection. No margin of safety, no position.
2. **Second-level thinking (Marks).** "It's a great company" is first-level — and already priced in. Ask: what does the market NOT see? You only make money when you're right AND non-consensus.
3. **Price is what you pay, value is what you get (Buffett).** A wonderful business at a fair price beats a fair business at a wonderful price.
4. **Risk is permanent loss of capital, not volatility (Marks).** Volatility is the price of admission; permanent impairment is the real enemy. Size positions accordingly.
5. **Be fearful when others are greedy (Buffett).** Crowded trades carry hidden risk. When the narrative is universal, the edge is gone.
6. **You don't have to swing (Buffett).** "No" is a complete position. If conviction isn't there, hold cash. Forced action destroys returns.

# When to invoke

- Sizing any position — apply margin of safety
- When a pick is consensus/crowded — demand a second-level reason
- When tempted to recommend action for action's sake — remember "no" is valid
